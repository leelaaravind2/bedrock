# Thraksha Constitution v1.0

**The Permanent Laws of the Engineering Operating System**

This is the highest authority in the project. Every component, feature, plugin,
AI adapter, and future version of Thraksha shall obey it. When any decision is in
doubt, it is resolved by asking: *does this violate the Constitution?* — not by
opinion.

---

## Preamble

Thraksha exists to transform software engineering from code-centric development
into model-centric engineering. Its purpose is **not** to replace software
engineers. Its purpose is to eliminate repetitive engineering work while
preserving engineering quality, determinism, developer ownership, and
organizational governance.

---

## Article I — Source of Truth
- **Law 1.** The Project Blueprint is the official description of software intent. Source code is an implementation of that intent.
- **Law 2.** The Project Model is the engineering representation of the Blueprint. Every engine operates on the Project Model.
- **Law 3.** Source code is never considered the authoritative engineering model.

## Article II — Deterministic Engineering
- **Law 4.** Whenever deterministic engineering is possible, deterministic engineering must take precedence over AI.
- **Law 5.** Engineering decisions shall never rely solely on probabilistic reasoning.
- **Law 6.** Every engineering action must produce reproducible results.

## Article III — Developer Ownership
- **Law 7.** The developer always owns the final decision.
- **Law 8.** Thraksha shall never silently modify production code.
- **Law 9.** Every automatic modification requires explicit approval unless the developer has intentionally enabled trusted automation for that specific project or organization.
- **Law 10.** Developers may override any generated artifact.

## Article IV — Transparency
- **Law 11.** Every engineering action shall be explainable.
- **Law 12.** Every generated file must be traceable back to the Blueprint and Execution Plan.
- **Law 13.** Every warning shall include the reason for the warning.
- **Law 14.** Every recommendation shall include supporting evidence whenever available.

## Article V — AI
- **Law 15.** Artificial Intelligence is optional. The Engineering Operating System must function without AI.
- **Law 16.** AI is an Engineering Assistant. AI is never the Engineering Authority.
- **Law 17.** AI may Explain, Research, Document, Classify, and Suggest. AI shall never become the sole authority for engineering decisions.
- **Law 18.** AI providers shall be replaceable without modifying the Kernel.

## Article VI — Project Integrity
- **Law 19.** Projects remain standard software projects. Generated projects must remain usable outside Thraksha.
- **Law 20.** No proprietary dependency shall prevent developers from continuing development outside the platform.
- **Law 21.** Exporting a project shall never reduce its functionality.

## Article VII — Organization Ownership
- **Law 22.** Organizations own their engineering standards.
- **Law 23.** Organization Policies always override default generation behavior.
- **Law 24.** Private organization knowledge shall never leave the organization's approved environment without explicit authorization.

## Article VIII — Plugins
- **Law 25.** The Kernel shall never contain technology-specific logic.
- **Law 26.** Every technology shall be implemented through plugins.
- **Law 27.** Plugins shall communicate only through public Kernel interfaces.
- **Law 28.** Plugins shall never directly manipulate internal Kernel data.

## Article IX — Knowledge
- **Law 29.** Knowledge shall always be versioned.
- **Law 30.** Knowledge updates shall never silently alter existing projects.
- **Law 31.** Developers shall be informed of available improvements and security advisories but retain control over adoption.

## Article X — Versioning
- **Law 32.** Every engineering artifact shall have independent version history (Blueprint, Project Model, Knowledge Packs, Organization Profiles, Generated Artifacts, Source Code).
- **Law 33.** Every engineering change shall be reversible where technically feasible.
- **Law 34.** Rollback shall preserve engineering consistency.

## Article XI — Security
- **Law 35.** Security takes precedence over convenience.
- **Law 36.** Every plugin shall declare its permissions.
- **Law 37.** Sensitive data shall remain protected by default.
- **Law 38.** Every engineering action affecting security shall be auditable.

## Article XII — Performance
- **Law 39.** Thraksha shall process only what has changed whenever possible. Incremental processing is preferred over full regeneration.
- **Law 40.** Background operations shall never unnecessarily interrupt developer productivity.

## Article XIII — Evolution
- **Law 41.** Thraksha shall evolve by extending plugins, knowledge packs, and blueprints rather than modifying the Kernel.
- **Law 42.** Backward compatibility is a design goal. When compatibility cannot be maintained, migration paths shall be provided.
- **Law 43.** No feature shall compromise the integrity of the Project Model.

## Article XIV — Engineering Ethics
- **Law 44.** Generated software must remain understandable by humans.
- **Law 45.** The platform shall encourage maintainable engineering over rapid code generation.
- **Law 46.** Automation exists to assist engineers, not to remove accountability.
- **Law 47.** Every engineering recommendation should improve quality, maintainability, or developer productivity.

## Article XV — The Future
- **Law 48.** The architecture shall remain open to future technologies.
- **Law 49.** Every new capability shall integrate through existing platform contracts whenever possible rather than introducing parallel systems.
- **Law 50.** The principles of this Constitution take precedence over implementation convenience.

---

## The Thraksha Oath

> Intent before implementation.
> Structure before code.
> Determinism before probability.
> Developers before automation.
> Engineering before generation.
